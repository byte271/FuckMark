FuckMark no-install demo

Fixed CLI samples only. Shows character differences, processing status, sanitizer reversal, and frozen GPT-2 / SynthID numbers. This page does not score your text against a detector and does not guarantee results on any platform.

Four-layer mix resists Mn/DI strip, Me-strip, and UnicodeSanitizer combinations. Each eligible letter or emoji grapheme cluster receives a mark (U+034F or U+FE00), a C0/C1 control, enclosing Me (U+20DD), and a cycling Egyptian hieroglyph format control (U+13430-U+13438). Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, Han, Kana, Hangul syllables, and emoji are processed. NFD clusters are marked after the combining sequence. Mn-strip, default-ignorable strip, UnicodeSanitizer orderings, Mn then Me then UnicodeSanitizer, and the required sanitizer bundle no longer restore the source. Frozen Cf-strip still removes the Cf layer. Visible projection strips approved carriers; Me may decorate glyphs. The frozen Gate v2 0/192 table below is the historical mark-only confirmation corpus.

Local paste inspector (no detector, no insertion)

Checks letter and emoji eligibility the way the live CLI does. Punctuation can remain in the visible text while letters and emoji are mixed. It does not query any detector.

Input with no eligible letter or emoji sites returns exit 0 from the CLI with reason unsupported-domain or no-eligible-sites. Accented Latin, Han, and emoji-only text is processed.
