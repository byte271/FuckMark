FuckMark no-install demo

Fixed CLI samples plus a local FuckMark watermark scan. Shows character differences, processing status, sanitizer reversal, and frozen GPT-2 / SynthID numbers. The paste detector only looks for FuckMark insertion characters. It does not query GPT-2 or SynthID and does not guarantee results on any platform.

Five-layer mix resists Mn/DI strip, Me-strip, UnicodeSanitizer, and Cf-strip after UnicodeSanitizer. Each eligible letter or emoji grapheme cluster receives a mark (U+034F or U+FE00), a C0/C1 control, enclosing Me (U+20DD), a cycling Egyptian hieroglyph format control (U+13430-U+13438), and a cycling interlinear annotation control (U+FFF9-U+FFFB). Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, Han, Kana, Hangul syllables, and emoji are processed. NFD clusters are marked after the combining sequence. Mn-strip, default-ignorable strip, UnicodeSanitizer orderings, Mn then Me then UnicodeSanitizer, Mn then Me then UnicodeSanitizer then frozen Cf-strip, and the required sanitizer bundle no longer restore the source. UnicodeSanitizer turns annotation controls into spaces. Visible projection strips approved carriers; Me may decorate glyphs. The frozen Gate v2 0/192 table below is the historical mark-only confirmation corpus.

Local paste inspector (eligibility, no network)

Checks letter and emoji eligibility the way the live CLI does. Punctuation can remain in the visible text while letters and emoji are mixed. The local FuckMark scan above is the watermark check; this box is for site eligibility.

Input with no eligible letter or emoji sites returns exit 0 from the CLI with reason unsupported-domain or no-eligible-sites. Accented Latin, Han, and emoji-only text is processed.
