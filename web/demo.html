Fixed CLI-shaped samples plus a local FuckMark scan. Samples are transformed in this tab by the same engine as the Python product CLI. The paste detector only looks for FuckMark insertion characters. It does not query GPT-2 or SynthID.

Five-layer mix resists Mn/DI strip, Me-strip, UnicodeSanitizer, and Cf-strip after UnicodeSanitizer. Each eligible letter or emoji grapheme cluster receives a mark (U+034F or U+FE00), a C0/C1 control, enclosing Me (U+20DD), a cycling Egyptian hieroglyph format control (U+13430-U+13438), and a cycling interlinear annotation control (U+FFF9-U+FFFB).
